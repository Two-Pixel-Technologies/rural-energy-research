Research Projects

Agriculture

Modeling of Energy, Water and Crops linkages

First work to quantify water-energy-food nexus linkages using a generalized methodology. Provides crop-wise energy usage (kWh/ha) and water applied (mm) for irrigation systems in Maharashtra.

Energy Systems

Technical paradigms in electricity supply for irrigation pumps

First techno-economic comparison of various electricity supply models for irrigation through a systemic lens. Found that minimum 875 hours of pumping is required for offgrid solar pumps to be cost-effective.

Year: 2020

Energy Systems

Restructuring of Low Tension Distribution Network

New approach to restructure distribution networks to ensure good quality power supply. Feasibility study in Umbarda Village led to one-third cost reduction, improved voltage profile and even loading of transformers.

Year: 2020-2021

Energy Systems

Capacitor Usage with Irrigation Pumps

Low-cost, effective solution to improve power quality for irrigation. Capacitor usage leads to 5-10% voltage improvement and 5% energy loss reduction, substantially reducing transformer failure and tripping incidences.

Year: 2014-2024

Learn more →
Energy Systems

Load Management at Irrigation Distribution Transformer

App-based solution to schedule farmers' irrigation requirements to avoid transformer overloading during peak Rabi season. Successfully implemented at 11 locations across Vidarbha and Marathwada.

Year: 2021-2024

Agriculture

Irrigation Investment Indices

Study on profitability of different cropping patterns as a function of water sources. Investigated the economics of irrigation investment across Marathwada and Vidarbha regions. Findings show increasing irrigation intensity accompanies mostly decreasing return on investment.
