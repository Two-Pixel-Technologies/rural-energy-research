Research Projects

Explore our ongoing and completed research initiatives addressing critical challenges in rural energy, irrigation systems, and sustainable development.

Agriculture

Sustainable Farming Practices

Researching climate-resilient agricultural methods for small-scale farmers in drought-prone regions. Focus on organic farming, crop diversification, and traditional knowledge integration. Working with local communities to document and preserve traditional agricultural practices while introducing modern sustainable techniques.

Learn more →
Water Resources

Rural Water Security

Developing community-based water management systems and studying groundwater recharge techniques. Includes mapping of traditional water harvesting structures and evaluating their effectiveness in modern contexts. Research on sustainable water use practices for agricultural and domestic purposes.

Learn more →
Energy Systems

Agricultural Distribution Networks

Analyzing and restructuring low tension agricultural distribution networks for improved efficiency. Study of load management and transformer capacity optimization. Focus on reducing transmission losses and improving power quality for agricultural feeders in rural areas.

Learn more →
Irrigation

Efficient Irrigation Pumps

Research on efficient irrigation pumps in the context of pressurized irrigation systems. Techno-economic analysis and energy-water-crops linkage modeling. Evaluating the performance of modern pump technologies and their adoption barriers in rural farming communities.

Learn more →
Renewable Energy

Solar Agriculture Feeders

Implementation and analysis of solar-powered agricultural feeders. Study of rural solar PV plant performance and community impact assessment. Research on the viability of solar energy as a reliable power source for agricultural operations and its economic benefits for farmers.

Learn more →
Demand Modeling

Irrigation Energy Demand

Comprehensive modeling of irrigation energy demand patterns. Analysis of distribution transformer capacity requirements and load management strategies. Development of predictive models to optimize energy supply for agricultural operations based on seasonal and crop-specific requirements.

Learn more →
Policy

Energy Policy Research

Policy analysis and recommendations for agricultural energy systems. Engagement with MERC, ATMA, and state development organizations. Working on evidence-based policy briefs to inform government decisions on rural energy infrastructure and subsidy programs.

Learn more →
Education

UMA Engineering Education

Integration of engineering curriculum with development problems. Field-based learning through internships and workshops oriented to societal challenges. Creating opportunities for engineering students to engage with real-world rural development issues and contribute to sustainable solutions.

Learn more →
Assessment

Energy Consumption Studies

Detailed studies on agricultural energy consumption patterns and estimation methodologies. Solar rooftop potential assessment for rural applications. Developing comprehensive frameworks for measuring and predicting energy consumption in agricultural contexts.
