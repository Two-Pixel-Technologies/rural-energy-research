Project on Climate Resilient Agriculture - IIT Bombay (Energy Projects)

Duration: 2020 – 2024
Funding: World Bank & Dept. of Agriculture, Gov. of Maharashtra
Location: Maharashtra
Status: Completed

Project Overview

The 'Project on Climate Resilient Agriculture' (PoCRA) or Nanji Deshmukh Sanjeevani Krishi Prakalp is a project implemented by the Department of Agriculture, Government of Maharashtra, funded by the World Bank to enhance climate-resilience and profitability of smallholder farming systems. The first phase of this project was carried out from 2017 to 2024 in 5000 villages of 15 districts in the divisions of Marathwada and West Vidarbha.

IIT Bombay worked with PoCRA on solutions for reliable access to water, and electricity for irrigation. Prof. Milind Sohoni was the Principal Investigator for the duration of the project. The MoUs between IIT Bombay and PoCRA, reports and solutions submitted are available at https://www.cse.iitb.ac.in/~pocra/.

The energy team led by co-Principal Investigator Prof. Priya Jadhav worked on the project from 2020. The main reports and solutions of the energy team are listed below.

Research Objectives

Our research focused on developing sustainable solutions for agricultural practices addressing both immediate farmer needs and long-term sustainability goals:

  • Develop climate-resilient agricultural practices and technologies
  • Ensure reliable access to water and electricity for irrigation
  • Improve energy efficiency in agricultural operations
  • Enhance power distribution infrastructure for rural areas
  • Build farmer capacity through training and knowledge transfer

Key Research Areas

The PoCRA energy projects encompassed several interconnected research initiatives addressing critical challenges in agricultural energy systems:

Restructuring of Low Tension Agricultural Distribution Networks: This component focused on improving the efficiency and reliability of agricultural electricity supply systems, reducing transmission losses, and ensuring adequate power supply for irrigation.

Efficient Irrigation Pumps - Context of Pressurized Irrigation: Research on optimizing pump selection, installation, and operation for pressurized irrigation systems including drip and sprinkler systems, leading to significant energy and water savings.

Modelling of Energy - Water - Crops Linkage: Development of integrated models to understand and optimize the complex relationships between energy use, water availability, and crop production under various climate scenarios.

Demand Modelling of Irrigation Energy Demand: Analysis and modeling of agricultural energy consumption patterns to inform infrastructure planning and policy decisions.

Capacitors and Load Management: Research on power quality improvement and load management strategies for agricultural feeders to enhance system reliability and efficiency.

Impact & Outreach

Beyond academic research, this project emphasized translating findings into practical applications. The team conducted extensive fieldwork across 5000 villages, engaging directly with farmers to understand their challenges and test solutions. Regular training workshops were conducted for farmers and agricultural officers, and policy briefs were submitted to government departments for scaling successful interventions.

The project brought together a multidisciplinary team of researchers, students, and field engineers to address complex challenges at the intersection of energy, water, and agriculture. The research outputs have influenced policy decisions and implementation strategies in Maharashtra's agricultural sector.
