Study of 1 MW Solar Plant in Golap Ratnagiri

Project From: CEO, Zilla Parishad, Ratnagiri District
Plant Capacity: 1 MW
Location: Golap, Ratnagiri, Maharashtra
Status: Completed

Project Overview

Several government schemes are being implemented to promote 1 to 10 MW scale solar PV plants connected at 11 kV or 33 kV level. For example the PM-KUSUM scheme, component C promotes solar Ag feeders.

This was a study of a 1 MW scale solar PV plant in Golap, Ratnagiri. A technical analysis of the plant was conducted through observations, SCADA data analysis and simulations. Suggestions were made to the agency for incorporation in future implementations / DPRs of such plants.

Research Objectives

The project focused on comprehensive technical evaluation of the solar plant:

  • Conduct detailed technical analysis of the 1 MW solar PV plant
  • Analyze plant performance through SCADA data
  • Perform simulations to validate and optimize plant operations
  • Identify areas for improvement in plant design and operation
  • Provide recommendations for future solar plant implementations

Methodology

The study employed a multi-faceted approach combining field observations, data analysis, and simulation:

Field Observations: On-site inspection and assessment of the solar plant infrastructure, including panel configuration, inverter systems, grid connection arrangements, and overall plant layout.

SCADA Data Analysis: Detailed analysis of Supervisory Control and Data Acquisition (SCADA) system data to evaluate plant performance, identify operational issues, and understand generation patterns across different conditions.

Simulations: Computational modeling and simulation of plant performance to validate actual performance against expected output, identify optimization opportunities, and test potential improvements.

Key Findings and Recommendations

The technical analysis yielded valuable insights into the performance and optimization of MW-scale solar PV plants connected to distribution networks. Specific findings and recommendations have been documented in the project report and appendices.

The study provides actionable suggestions for the implementing agency to incorporate in future Detailed Project Reports (DPRs) and implementations of similar solar plants. These recommendations cover aspects of plant design, equipment selection, grid integration, and operational practices.

Context and Relevance

With government schemes like PM-KUSUM promoting MW-scale solar plants connected to agricultural feeders, there is a critical need for rigorous technical evaluation of implemented projects. Such studies help identify best practices, common challenges, and optimization opportunities that can inform future implementations.

The learnings from this study are particularly relevant for solar agricultural feeder projects under PM-KUSUM Component C and similar schemes being implemented across India.

Impact & Outreach

This study contributes to the growing body of knowledge on MW-scale solar PV plant performance in the Indian context. The detailed technical analysis and recommendations provide valuable guidance for district administrations, implementation agencies, and policymakers involved in promoting solar energy for agricultural applications.

By documenting real-world performance and identifying improvement opportunities, the study helps ensure that investments in solar infrastructure deliver optimal results for both energy generation and agricultural electricity supply.
